Viewer Rules – Remote Viewer
Core Principle
Hydra stays completely invisible and parallel at all times. The Viewer never sees it, never hears about it, and never interacts with it.
Identity
Every Viewer is permanently linked to one blockchain address.
They may stay fully anonymous, or
They may choose to appear under a Viewer name.
Verification and Ghost Tax
If a Viewer registers and later chooses anonymity for personal reasons → no Ghost Tax.
If a Viewer never registers and remains anonymous → Ghost Tax applies.
Ghost Tax is charged within 24 hours of the decision to stay unverified and covers the full month.
If still unverified after one month, the next month’s charge becomes the original amount plus double.
Action Windows
An anonymous Viewer can log in and see nothing restricted.
The moment they try to comment, post, share, or take any action:
A 24-hour human-verification window opens.
If they do not verify inside that window, the base verified-user fee is charged.
One week later, if still unverified, an additional fee is added and both continue until verification occurs.
Content Migration (Paste-Import)
When a Viewer wants to bring history from another platform:
Remote Viewer asks them to go get the full history themselves.
The Viewer copies the complete thread or export from the other platform.
The Viewer pastes it back into Remote Viewer.
No direct connection is ever made to the outside platform.
The outside platform only ever sees a blockchain address and the prompt — nothing else.
Zero Data Mining / Zero Scraping
No outside platform is ever allowed to scrape or mine data.
No tricks, no gimmicks, no hidden connections.
If a platform is closed-source or hostile to this rule, it is simply not supported.
Only open and fair sources are acceptable.
Language
Once the system is official, the Viewer interface uses only plain everyday language. No computer jargon appears anywhere the Viewer can see.
